Variax Workbench and Rig Check — Windows x64 offline alpha

Extract the whole ZIP before opening either executable. No installer,
Line 6 driver or separate runtime is required.

The apps are unsigned. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected
your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway if you trust this download.
SHA256SUMS.txt lists the checksums of the extracted files. The download
also includes a separate SHA-256 checksum for the ZIP.

Variax Workbench opens and edits preset and library files offline.
In Rig Check, choose Practice for a simulated rig or This computer for
the Windows, USB inventory, offline engine and audio inventory checks.
The Windows connection isn't available in this version yet.

Rig Check reports are saved under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Rig Check\reports.
