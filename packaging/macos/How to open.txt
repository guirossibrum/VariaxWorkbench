Variax Workbench and Rig Check — untested macOS alpha

Drag both apps onto the Applications shortcut. Open each app from Applications.
These builds use an ad-hoc signature and have not been notarized.

macOS Catalina
Right-click the app, choose Open, then confirm Open.

Current macOS
Try to open the app once. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose
Open Anyway for that app.

Fallback for a copy you trust
In Terminal, use the app's actual name in place of <app>:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/<app>.app"

The Mac connection isn't available in this version yet. Variax Workbench edits
files offline. Rig Check offers practice mode and computer-only checks.
